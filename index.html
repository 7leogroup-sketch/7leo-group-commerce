About 7Leo Group

ABOUT 7LEO GROUP

7Leo Group is an independent digital vision built from Karnataka, bringing together creativity, commerce, music, technology, and a growing collection of digital experiences under one identity. 7Leo Group began from zero with a simple idea — to build something meaningful with the resources available. What started with a mobile phone, Wi-Fi, determination, and a dream is becoming a platform designed to grow across different creative and business directions. 7Leo Group is home to experiences such as 7Leo Store and 7Leo BGM, with more ideas and projects planned for the future. But 7Leo Group is bigger than any single project. It represents the journey of starting from nothing, learning along the way, facing challenges, and continuing forward without giving up. VISION — See beyond today. POWER — Keep moving through challenges. LEGACY — Build something that can live beyond the beginning. 7Leo Group is not just a website. It is a journey from ZERO toward something greater. The journey has only begun. 7LEO GROUP VISION. POWER. LEGACY.

ABOUT 7LEO STORE

7Leo Store is the official commerce experience of 7Leo Group, created to bring together premium lifestyle products, fashion, accessories, and selected everyday essentials. Our vision is simple — Build to Buy. 7Leo Store is designed around a modern, premium shopping experience with carefully presented products, order handling, fulfillment support, order tracking, and a smooth customer journey. Through our supplier and fulfillment network, products can be sourced and delivered while maintaining the 7Leo Group vision of quality, style, innovation, and legacy. 7Leo Store — Build to Buy.

ABOUT 7LEO BGM

7Leo BGM is the music experience of 7Leo Group, created for original mobile-composed music and creative soundscapes. Built around mobile music creation and Groovepad-inspired composition, 7Leo BGM explores rhythm, atmosphere, energy, and creativity through original musical ideas. Every soundscape represents another side of the 7Leo Group vision — creative freedom, experimentation, and artistic expression. 7Leo BGM — Feel the Sound. Create the Legacy.

Founder Story

7Leo Group was not built from a perfect beginning. It was built from a dream. With no financial support, no business team, no friends beside me, and no laptop — I started with only a mobile phone, Wi-Fi, and the determination to create something of my own. The journey was not easy. There were difficulties, limitations, doubts, and moments when giving up would have been the easier choice. But I kept going. Every step was taken from zero. Every idea was built with patience. Every challenge became another reason to learn, improve, and continue. 7Leo Group represents that journey — from starting with almost nothing to building a vision for something greater. This is not a story of having everything. It is a story of starting with what you have. Sometimes you have to walk alone before others understand where you are going. I may have started alone, but I believe a strong vision can become bigger than the person who started it. 7Leo Group is my journey of Vision, Power, and Legacy. The journey has only begun. — ABUBAKKER BELLARY Founder, 7Leo Group
