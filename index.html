LEGAL & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

Indian Dropshipping Business Guidelines

Operating as an Indian intermediary dropshipping merchant entails adherence to GST regulations, consumer protection mandates, and supplier terms.

GST & Taxation (India)

As an Indian seller, GST registration (GSTIN) is mandatory for interstate e-commerce sales. Tax is applicable on the taxable value (margin or total sale price depending on composite/regular scheme). Professional CA verification is recommended.

Supplier & Fulfillment Terms

Orders are fulfilled through verified third-party partners (Alibaba, AliExpress, Amazon). 7Leo Store acts as the merchant of record while suppliers manage packaging, courier transit, and international customs clearance.

Consumer Protection & Returns

Merchants are legally accountable for customer satisfaction and refund processing under Indian Consumer Protection Act guidelines, coordinating with suppliers for physical item returns when applicable.
