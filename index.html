About 7Leo Group

ABOUT 7LEO GROUP

7Leo Group is an independent digital vision built from Karnataka, bringing together creativity, commerce, music, technology, and a growing collection of digital experiences under one identity. 7Leo Group began from zero with a simple idea — to build something meaningful with the resources available. What started with a mobile phone, Wi-Fi, determination, and a dream is becoming a platform designed to grow across different creative and business directions. 7Leo Group is home to experiences such as 7Leo Store, 7Leo BGM, and 7Leo Star, with more ideas and projects planned for the future.
